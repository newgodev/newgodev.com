Writing hints — Internet addiction

Hints only · use your own mind-map words · >150 words · skim <1 min

Do not copy this as your essay. Fix slots on your paper, then write in your words.

1 · Intro (~2–3 sentences)

  • Sentence 1: what internet addiction is (can’t stop / too much time online).
  • Sentence 2: pick one cause from your paper as a hook — exam/HW stress · family pressure · escape real life · scrolling long hours · online self-esteem.
  • Link with As a result of… or the reason why… → why the problem matters (school / sleep / family). Still only 1 cause.
  • Last line: preview causes, effects (individual + society), and what can be done.
  • Do not dump all causes here.

2 · Body — types / causes

Your B

  • Core: Difficult away from online + preoccupation (≈ keep thinking about).
  • Example under that: playing / chat at online games (one example, not two equal causes).
  • less caution → fold into “hard to stay away” or drop.
  • Poor interact at real world → move to effects / society, not a type.
  • Slot each para: Opinion → Reason → Example → Opinion.
  • Try: The reason why… / As a result of… playing / chat…
  • Optional one-line link from your causes: exam/HW stress or escape real life → hard to stay away.

3 · Body — effects

Your C

  • Chain order: lose control of gaming → bad sleep → late school → bad result → health / eye / hearing.
  • Individual: sleep, health/eyes, lose control, can’t focus, mood, loss time/weight.
  • Society: poor real-world interact (+ loss work if you use While-viewing). One society example is enough.
  • Don’t repeat sleep twice.
  • Link: …lead to late school… / …and, in turn, bad result

4 · Help the addict

Your D / cure

  • Use: limit apps · parent control · timetable · unplugged / nature.
  • For each: say which effect it helps (sleep? school? control?) — don’t only list names.

5 · Conclusion

  • Start: In conclusion / To conclude / To sum up
  • Restate main points only (problem → key cause/effect → help). No new ideas.

Useful phrases

As a result of… · cause / lead to · the reason why… · …and, in turn,…
