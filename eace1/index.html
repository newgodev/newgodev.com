Cause & effect — words to highlight

Yellow = cause · Blue = effect signal · Green = link phrases (use these in your writing)

cause effect / result linking phrase

1 · Useful phrases (like “as a result of”)

  • as a result of + cause → effect
  • because of / due to + cause
  • one reason why … is that …
  • leads to / results in / causes
  • therefore / so / consequently
  • in turn
  • as a result, …

Tip: put a cause before the green phrase, and an effect after it (or the other way for “because of”).

2 · Your draft — highlight the link words

Title: Internet addiction causes and effects

One reason why this happens is that people escape real life. Then, I will talk about causes, effects and how to help.

First of all, for individuals it is difficult to stay away from online preoccupation. For example, Benedict was playing at online games and found it hard to stay away from it. This leads to poor interacting with people in the real world. It also leads to bad sleep and late for school.

Secondly, for society it causes families to argue and loss of work, and in turn, people in general talk less face to face.

However, I will suggest some things that can be done to help the addict. For example, add a time limit for your apps or games. Also, unplug and go outside to meet people face to face. These tips might help you control your own time and unwind your body.

In conclusion, internet addiction is a preoccupation for individuals and family arguments for society. Also, control your own time by adding a time limit on apps or games.
