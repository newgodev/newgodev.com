Title: Internet addiction causes and effects

Internet addiction is too much time online. One reason why this happens is that people escape real life. Then I will talk about causes, effects and how to help.

First of all, for individuals it is difficult to stay away from online preoccupation. For example, Benedict was playing at online games and found it hard to stay away from it. This leads to poor interact at real world. It also leads to bad sleep and late school.

Secondly, for society it causes family argue and loss of work, and in turn, people in general talk less face to face.

However, I will suggest some things that can be done to help the addict. For example, add limit time for your apps or games. Also, unplug and go outside to meet people face to face. These tips might help you control your own time and unwind your body.

In conclusion, internet addiction is a preoccupation for individuals and family argue for society. Also, control your own time by adding limited time on apps or games.
